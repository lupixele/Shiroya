# Aptitude – III Semester | Complete Quantitative & Reasoning Study Notes

Comprehensive, standalone aptitude study modules covering all 12 Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning topics for Aditya University CRT / III Semester.

Each module is self-contained with complete theoretical foundations, universal formulas, fast mental calculation tricks, and an exhaustive question bank featuring full problem statements, multiple-choice options, bolded correct answers, detailed step-by-step mathematical explanations, and exam shortcuts.

## Course Modules & Study Guides

1. **[01. Time and Work](topics/01-time-and-work.md)**
   - Core concepts: Work-rate equivalence, efficiency inverse laws, cyclic combos, alternate days, worker leaving/joining mechanics, chain rule, and wage division.
   - Question bank: 30 verified problems with unitary, LCM unit, and speed shortcut methods.

2. **[02. Pipes and Cisterns](topics/02-pipes-and-cisterns.md)**
   - Core concepts: Inlets vs. outlets, net throughput, LCM capacity method, pipe cross-section scaling ($Q \propto d^2$), alternating taps, and multi-liquid proportions.
   - Question bank: 30 verified problems solved with capacity allocation.

3. **[03. Mixture and Alligation](topics/03-mixture-and-alligation.md)**
   - Core concepts: Alligation cross rule, weighted average formulations, repeated dilution/replacement ($x(1 - y/x)^n$), and invariant component method.
   - Question bank: 30 verified problems solved via both algebraic and alligation cross methods.

4. **[04. Partnership](topics/04-partnership.md)**
   - Core concepts: Investment-time-profit relation ($\text{Profit} \propto \text{Capital} \times \text{Time}$), simple vs. compound partnerships, working partner management allowances, and pasture/rental sharing.
   - Question bank: 33 verified problems with full ratio derivations.

5. **[05. Permutations and Combinations](topics/05-permutations-and-combinations.md)**
   - Core concepts: Fundamental Counting Principles, factorials, linear arrangements ($n\text{P}r$, repeated elements), combinations ($n\text{C}r$), block method, gap method, circular permutations, and geometric combinations.
   - Question bank: 35 verified problems with factorial expansions and combinatorial logic.

6. **[06. Quadratic Equations](topics/06-quadratic-equations.md)**
   - Core concepts: Nature of roots via discriminant ($D = b^2 - 4ac$), root relation identities, master Fast Sign Method for 5-second root finding, and 4-cell pairwise comparison grids ($x > y, x < y, x \ge y, x \le y, x = y \text{ or CND}$).
   - Question bank: 30 comprehensive comparison and radical problems.

7. **[07. Ranking and Sequence](topics/07-ranking-and-sequence.md)**
   - Core concepts: Total strength ($T = L + R - 1$), non-overlapping vs. overlapping between cases, position interchange dynamics, extrema, and conditional symbol streams.
   - Question bank: 35 verified problems with step-by-step indexing.

8. **[08. Seating Arrangement](topics/08-seating-arrangement.md)**
   - Core concepts: Linear facing North/South, double parallel rows, circular arrangements (inward, outward, mixed), polygonal tables, and multi-storey floor puzzles.
   - Question bank: 16 full puzzle sets (50+ sub-questions) with ASCII spatial layout diagrams and step-by-step constraint elimination.

9. **[09. Puzzles, Numbers and Symbols](topics/09-puzzles-numbers-and-symbols.md)**
   - Core concepts: Mathematical operator encoding, BODMAS evaluation, day/week scheduling, box stacks, radial spokes, geometric missing numbers, and alphanumeric series.
   - Question bank: 30 verified puzzle problems with deduction trails.

10. **[10. Data Interpretation](topics/10-data-interpretation.md)**
    - Core concepts: Percentages, growth rates, ratios, averages, pie chart degree-to-percentage conversion ($1\% = 3.6^\circ$), and mental fraction approximations.
    - Question bank: 10 full datasets (tables, pie charts, clustered bar charts, line graphs) reconstructed into clean Markdown tables, with 30 solved analytical questions.

11. **[11. Data Sufficiency](topics/11-data-sufficiency.md)**
    - Core concepts: The 5 standard decision options (A through E), 3-step decision flow, value vs. binary yes/no criteria, statement independence, and avoiding over-calculation.
    - Question bank: 30 verified problems spanning quantitative, algebraic, and logical domains.

12. **[12. Clocks and Calendars](topics/12-clocks-and-calendars.md)**
    - Core concepts: Angular hand speeds, relative speed ($5.5^\circ/\text{min}$), clock angle formula ($\theta = |30H - 5.5M|$), faulty clocks (gain/loss), odd days arithmetic, century boundary constraints, and calendar recurrence cycles ($+28, +6, +11$).
    - Question bank: 55 verified problems with clock angle formulas and calendar date determinations.
