- Introduction
- Time and work are a fundamental arithmetic concept used to determine the relationship between the time taken to finish a task and the efficiency of the person or machine performing it. It is based on the idea that the total work done is the result of the rate of work multiplied by the total time spent

## Slide 03

### On-slide text

- 3
- Concept and Definitions
- Given below are a few such important time and work formulas for your reference:
- Work Done = Time Taken × Rate of Work
- Rate of Work = 1 / Time Taken
- Time Taken = 1 / Rate of Work
- If a piece of work is done in x number of days, then the work done in one day = 1/x
- Total Work Done = Number of Days × Efficiency
- Efficiency and Time are inversely proportional to each other

## Slide 04

### On-slide text

- 4
- Key Formulae
- 1) If a pipe requires 'x' hours to fill up the tank, then part filled in 1 hr = 1/x
- 2) If a pipe requires 'y' hours to empty the full tank, then part emptied in 1 hr =1/y
- 3) Net Work Done = (Sum of work done by inlets) - (Sum of work done by outlets)
- 4) Suppose that one pipe takes 'x' hours to fill up the tank and another pipe takes 'y' hours to empty the full tank, then, on opening both the pipes, there are 2 possible conditions:
- For condition I: x < y, Net part filled in 1 hr = 1/x – 1/y
- For condition II: x > y, Net part emptied in 1 hr = 1/y – 1/x
