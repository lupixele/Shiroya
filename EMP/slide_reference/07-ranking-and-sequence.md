## Slide 02

### On-slide text

- Introduction
- Ranking: Finding the position of a person/object from the left/right or top/bottom in a linear row.
- Sequence: Arranging a group of people based on comparisons like height, weight, age, or marks using inequalities (> or <).
- When analyzing two different people in the same row, calculate the sum of their positions (Left + Right) and compare it to the Total:
- Case 1: Non-Overlapping (Sum is less than Total)
- Between = Total - (Left + Right)
- Case 2: Overlapping (Sum is greater than Total)
- Between = (Left + Right) - Total - 2
- Total People in a Row:
- Total = Left + Right - 1
- Finding a Position from an End:
- Left = Total - Right + 1

## Slide 03

### On-slide text

- Two Persons: Finding the Number of People Between Them
- Use these when you know the positions of two different people (Person A from the Left, Person B from the Right and the Total (T).
- First, calculate the sum of their positions: Sum = LA + RB.
- Case 1: Non-Overlapping (Simple Row)
- Use this if the total space is larger than the sum of their positions Sum < T).
- People Between = T - (LA + RB )
- Case 2: Overlapping (Crossed Over)
- Use this if the two people cross paths (Sum > T).
- People Between = (LA + RB) - T - 2

## Slide 04

### On-slide text

- Maximum and Minimum Strengths
- Sometimes a question asks for the maximum or minimum possible number of people in a row given two positions (LA and RB) and the number of people between them (N).
- Maximum Strength of the Row:	Occurs in a non-overlapping arrangement.
- Max = LA + RB + N
- Minimum Strength of the Row:	Occurs in an overlapping arrangement.
- Min = LA + RB - N - 2
- (Note: For a valid minimum condition, the value of Min must be greater than or equal to the larger of the two individual ranks).

## Slide 05

### On-slide text

- Position Interchanging Formulas
- Use these when two people, A and B, swap their places in a row.
- New Position of Second Person (Bnew):	If A's position shifts by a certain number of places, B's position shifts by the exact same number.			Bnew = Bold + Anew - Bold
- Total Number of People ( T ) after Interchanging:		Take the new position of one person and add the old position of the other person, then subtract 1.		T = Anew + Bold - 1   		T = Bnew + Aold - 1
