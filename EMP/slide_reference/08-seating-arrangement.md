## Slide 02

### On-slide text

- 2
- Introduction
- Different Types of Seating Arrangement Problems:
- 1. Linear Arrangement: This type requires arranging individuals in a straight line, usually forming a single row. The main goal is to organize everyone one after another along this line.
- 2. Double-Row Arrangement: In these cases, two separate groups are placed in two rows facing each other. One group sits in one row, and the other group is positioned directly opposite in the second row, creating a face-to-face seating layout.
- 3. Circular arrangement: In circular seating arrangement questions, you need to arrange persons around a circular table, fulfilling certain conditions.4. Rectangular arrangement: These arrangements are very similar to circular ones; the only difference is that persons sit around a rectangular table.

## Slide 04

### On-slide text

- 4
- Direction for circular arrangement: In case of circular arrangement questions, or rectangular arrangement, the persons may be facing the centre of the circle or they may be looking away from the center.Circular (Towards center): If people are looking towards the centre, then the right hand side will be in the anticlockwise direction and left hand side will be in the clockwise directionCircular (Away from center):If the people are looking away from the centre then the right hand side will be in the clockwise direction and left hand side will be in the anti clockwise direction.
- Some hints for solving sitting arrangement problems: • Information regarding position of players is important. • If A is sitting next to B, then it means that A and B are sitting together. B may be to the right or left of A. • If B is sitting to the right/left of A, then it does not mean that B is sitting immediate right/left of A. There may be some other persons sitting between A and B. • If B is sitting immediate right/left of A then it will be mentioned in the statement of the question.
