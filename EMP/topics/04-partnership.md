## 1. Theory and formulas

### 1.1 Capital × time
Partners share profit or loss based on **investment multiplied by the amount of time it is invested**.

**Formula: Partner's weight = Capital × Time**.

**Profit share = Total profit × (partner's capital-time)/(sum of all capital-times)**.

### 1.2 Simple and compound partnership
- **Simple:** Everyone invests for the same time; profit ratio = capitals ratio.
- **Compound:** Times differ or investments change; add the capital-time products across each period.

### 1.3 Given ratios / inverse time calculation
If investment ratio A:B = `a:b` and time ratio A:B=`x:y`, then **profit ratio = ax:by**. If profits and investments are known, **time ratio = profit ratio ÷ investment ratio** (term-wise).

## 2. Questions and answers
**Q1 (PPT).** Investment ratio 3:4, time ratio 4:5. Profit ratio?
**Answer:** `3×4 : 4×5 =12:20=3:5`.

**Q2 (PPT).** Investments 2:3:4; times 1:2:3. Profit ratio?
**Answer:** `2:6:12=1:3:6`.

**Q3 (PPT).** A invests ₹10,000; B ₹15,000 for same 1 year; profit ₹20,000. Shares?
**Answer:** ratio 2:3; A=`20000×2/5=₹8000`, B=`₹12000`.

**Q4 (PPT).** A's capital=2×B's; B's=3×C's; total ₹20,000. A's share?
**Answer:** capitals `6:3:1`; A=`6/10×20000=₹12,000`.

**Q5 (PPT).** 4A=6B=10C (capitals); total profit ₹4,650. C share?
**Answer:** `A:B:C=1/4:1/6:1/10=15:10:6`. C=`4650×6/31=₹900`.

**Q6 (additional).** A invests ₹5000 for 12 months; B ₹8000 for 6 months. Profit ₹9000. Shares?
**Answer:** weights=60000:48000=5:4; shares **₹5000 and ₹4000**.

## 3. Tricks and tips
1. Always multiply money × months before reducing the ratio.
2. If a partner changes capital, **split into periods** and sum the capital-months.
3. With fixed total profit, **sum of shares must equal total**.
4. Same-time partnership is an ordinary ratio problem.

## 4. Additional exam notes
PPT also uses sharing car hire by hours and pasture charges by animals × months: these use the same contribution/proportion principle. **Source discrepancy:** One PPT question states investments ₹10,000, ₹15,000, ₹12,000, but its printed solution uses 10:15:20; do not copy that solution without checking the actual question values.
