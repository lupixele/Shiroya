## 1. Theory and formulas

### 1.1 Meaning of a quadratic equation
A quadratic has form **ax² + bx + c = 0**, where **a ≠ 0**. Its **roots** are values of x that make the equation zero.

### 1.2 Factorization
Find two numbers with product `ac` and sum `b`, then factor if possible.

**Zero-product rule:** If `(x−p)(x−q)=0`, then **x=p or x=q**.

### 1.3 Quadratic formula
**x = [−b ± √(b²−4ac)]/(2a)**.

**Discriminant Δ = b²−4ac**:
- Δ > 0: two different real roots.
- Δ = 0: one repeated real root.
- Δ < 0: no real roots (two complex roots).

### 1.4 Relations between roots
If roots are α and β, **α+β = −b/a** and **αβ = c/a**. An equation with roots α,β is **x² − (α+β)x + αβ = 0** (or any nonzero multiple).

### 1.5 Comparing roots in two equations
Solve both and compare the whole **sets** of possible values, not just one root. If all roots of x-equation are larger than all roots of y-equation, x>y; if relations vary, the comparison cannot be uniquely determined.

## 2. Questions and answers
**Q1 (additional).** Solve x²−5x+6=0.
**Answer:** `(x−2)(x−3)=0`; **x=2 or 3**.

**Q2 (additional).** Solve 2x²−8x+8=0.
**Answer:** divide by 2: `(x−2)²=0`; **x=2** repeated.

**Q3 (additional).** Solve x²−2x−3=0 using formula.
**Answer:** a=1,b=−2,c=−3, Δ=16. `x=(2±4)/2`, so **x=3 or −1**.

**Q4 (additional).** Roots sum=7, product=10. Form an equation.
**Answer:** `x²−7x+10=0`.

**Q5 (additional).** Compare roots of x²−5x+6=0 and y²−9y+20=0.
**Answer:** x∈{2,3}; y∈{4,5}. Therefore **x<y** in every case.

## 3. Tricks and tips
1. Move everything to **one side = 0** first.
2. Check factor pairs before using the quadratic formula.
3. For coefficient 1, find two numbers whose **sum=b** and **product=c** (account for signs).
4. A repeated root appears when **Δ=0**.
