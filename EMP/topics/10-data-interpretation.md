## 1. Theory and formulas

### 1.1 Data interpretation (DI)
DI converts numeric information from **tables, bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, caselets and logical data sets** into answers. Extract the correct labels and units *before* calculating.

### 1.2 Basic formulas
- **Total = sum of entries**.
- **Difference = larger − smaller** (if absolute difference is requested).
- **Average = total / number of entries**.
- **Percentage = part/whole × 100**.
- **Part from percent = total × percent/100**.
- **Percent change = (new − old)/old × 100**.
- **Ratio A:B = A/B**, reduce both to simplest whole-number ratio.
- **Pie angle = part/total × 360°**; **value of sector = total × angle/360°**.

### 1.3 Tabular DI
Identify the requested row, column and time window. Sum only relevant intersections; check whether units are counts, money, percentage, or thousands.

### 1.4 Pie-chart DI
All sectors total **100%** or **360°**. Multiplying percent by the known overall total yields the sector's value.

### 1.5 Line/bar chart DI
Read category, series/legend, vertical-axis scale and unit. Distinguish 'increase by' from 'increased to'.

### 1.6 Caselet/logical DI
Build a small table from prose before computing. Use the given relationships, avoiding assumptions about missing categories.

## 2. Questions and answers
**Q1 (PPT).** Book sales March=180, April=210, May=240. Total?
**Answer:** `180+210+240=630 books`.

**Q2 (PPT).** January=120 and May=240. Difference?
**Answer:** `240−120=120 books`.

**Q3 (PPT).** January=120, February=150, March=180. Average?
**Answer:** `(120+150+180)/3=150`.

**Q4 (PPT).** Family income ₹1,00,000; pie chart A=35%, C=40%. Total A+C?
**Answer:** `(35+40)%×100000=₹75,000`.

**Q5 (PPT).** Same chart B=20%, C=40%, D=5%. Total B+C+D?
**Answer:** `65%×100000=₹65,000`.

**Q6 (PPT).** Same chart average income for four people?
**Answer:** `₹1,00,000/4=₹25,000`.

**Q7 (PPT).** Line graph CSE in colleges A–E: 320,280,240,300,220. Total CSE?
**Answer:** `320+280+240+300+220=1360`.

**Q8 (additional).** Sales rise from 200 to 250. Increase percent?
**Answer:** `(250−200)/200×100=25%`.

## 3. Tricks and tips
1. **Read the legend and axis scale** before extracting numbers.
2. Keep unit conversions consistent: 10 thousand ≠ 10.
3. For a pie chart, 10%=36°, 25%=90°, 50%=180°.
4. Estimate first to catch arithmetic errors.
